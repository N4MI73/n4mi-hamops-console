STATION & PROFILE

Data source: Home reads PropMon and the home weather station in the shack. Field uses this Pi and the internet (a phone hotspot). You switch it; the console never switches by itself.

Station: Home and Field each keep their own callsign and grid. In Field Mode use a 6-character grid: a 4-character square is too big for NWS alerts. GPS (Field only) sets the grid once the same square has held for 2 minutes.

Operating profile: Field Day adds the timer to the status bar and, in Field Mode, rates the Field Day bands for North America. Alerts are the same in both.

Field Day times are in UTC, set with the picker. Setup start left blank means local midnight on the operations day.

DISPLAY

Main clock: UTC or local shown first everywhere. Tapping the big time in the status bar swaps them too.

Brightness: one level by day, one at night (between the night times, local). Red night style turns the screen red to protect night vision; alerts then rely on their words and the flashing border.

Panel cycling: Home, Propagation and Weather in turn. A touch pauses it; a critical alert holds Home until acknowledged.

WI-FI

When several saved networks are in range, the one highest in the list is joined. The arrows change the order; Forget takes two taps. Join a nearby network and type its password on the keyboard.

DATA SOURCES

Home addresses: PropMon and Weather Sentinel on the home network. Test connections tries each one, and NWS, and says what answered.

Weather radio list: stored on this Pi for use with no internet. Update now at home before a field trip.

Field lightning detection: Full about 60 MB an hour, Economy about 20. It turns itself off when the NWS outlook clears. The at-home test compares the satellite with the Tempest, on the System page only.

SAVING

Every change is saved at once. A value that doesn't fit is refused with the reason, and nothing is saved.
