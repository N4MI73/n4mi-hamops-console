Help · SystemWhat this Pi, its connections and its data sources are doing
THIS PI

CPU temperature: amber "warm" from 75 °C, red "hot" from 80 °C, where the Pi starts to slow itself down. Check the fan and keep the cage out of the sun.

Power: red Under-voltage now means the supply can't keep up. Use the official 27 W supply, a short cable and the Jackery's AC outlet. Amber means it happened since the last start.

Disk names the drive the system is running from. Amber microSD SPARE means the NVMe didn't boot and the Pi started from the backup card: everything works, but settings and saved state are as of the last backup. Fix or replace the NVMe when you're home (see the backup guide).

Memory, load, uptime: for spotting trouble; normal is low.

NETWORK · LOCATION · TIME

Wi-Fi and Internet: which network and how strong. Internet "Reachable" means NWS answers. Join networks in Settings → Wi-Fi.

Location: where the grid comes from: Manual, GPS, or "waiting for GPS" (typed grid in use). Amber "not yet confirmed" means the last GPS grid from before a restart, until the next fix.

GPS: fix and satellites. Details › (or tap the GPS light) opens the sky view.

Clock: internet time, or the backup clock (RTC) with no internet. "GPS agrees" is a cross-check only: the console never sets its clock from GPS. Backup clock: the RTC battery that keeps time while powered off.

Put the GPS receiver outside with open sky; indoors it may hear satellites without a fix.

DATA SOURCES

One line per source, with a dot: green current, amber old, red unavailable, and the reason when it isn't green.

Home Mode: PropMon and the home weather station on the home network. Field Mode: HamQSL and NOAA over the internet, and NWS for the Field grid.

Weather radio list: age of the stored transmitter list. Update it at home before a field trip (Settings → Data sources → Update now).

Console service not answering. Data on screen may be old. NWS alerts: check NOAA Weather Radio.
