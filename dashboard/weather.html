Help · WeatherWhat this page shows and how to read it
THIS LOCATION

Lightning: detected lightning near the console. At home it comes from the home weather station (Tempest): amber within 10 mi, flashing red within 5 mi.

The 5-mile alert holds for 30 minutes after the last strike (the 30/30 rule). Clear ends it early if you know the storm has gone; the next strike restarts it.

Lightning possible / likely: an NWS forecast, not a detection. A reason to watch the sky and the radio.

NWS alerts: the worst active warning, watch or advisory for this spot, with when it ends.

A Severe Thunderstorm Warning is about wind and hail; lightning can come with any storm, warned or not.

NOW AND FORECAST

Now: at home, the home weather station. In Field Mode, the nearest NWS station (often an airport 10–30 mi away, updated about hourly).

Forecast: NWS periods and the next 12 hours, with temperature, lightning chance, rain chance and gusts (marked from 25 mph).

Weather Radio: the NOAA Weather Radio transmitter for this county, with its frequency and the county's SAME code for your radio. "Not listed for county" means the nearest one may not carry this county's warnings.

WHEN DATA IS MISSING OR OLD

STALE · N MIN: no fresh data for a while. Shown greyed; treat it as old.

NWS alerts out of date / unavailable: the console can't check NWS just now. Not the same as no alerts: listen to NOAA Weather Radio.

Home weather station in test mode: the station is being bench-tested; its alerts and lightning are ignored until it finishes.

HOME AND FIELD

Home Mode uses the home weather station and home grid; Field Mode uses NWS for the Field grid (typed, or from GPS).

Console service not answering. Data on screen may be old. NWS alerts: check NOAA Weather Radio.
