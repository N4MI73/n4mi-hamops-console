SOLAR AND GEOMAGNETIC

SFI (solar flux): higher helps the upper bands.

K index: geomagnetic unrest, with NOAA's storm scale: 0–3 quiet, 4 unsettled, 5 storm G1, up to 9 storm G5. Storms close the higher bands first.

X-ray: solar flares. M and X flares can black out HF on the daylight side for minutes to hours (R1–R4).

Figures in amber are high enough to hurt conditions.

Storm recovery (amber line): for 12 hours after a storm the ratings are held down, even once the K index has dropped.

FIELD DAY

Ratings describe band conditions for North American contacts, not a mode: they hold for SSB and CW too, though part of the data is FT8 reports (no FT8 or FT4 at Winter Field Day). Not yet checked against June.

WHEN DATA IS MISSING OR OLD

STALE · N MIN: no fresh ratings for 15 minutes or more. The page greys out; treat the ratings as old.

Band ratings unavailable: solar flux or the K index can't be had just now, so the bands show "--" rather than guesses.

No data yet: PropMon has just restarted. Ratings return within a few minutes.

HOME AND FIELD

Home Mode shows PropMon's ratings for the home grid square, wherever the console is. Field Mode rates the Field grid square itself, and the Field Day profile switches to North America reach.
