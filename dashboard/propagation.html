BAND RATINGS

GOOD · FAIR · POOR for each band, right now:

GOOD: DX is workable on SSB.
FAIR: DX on FT8 only, or North America only on SSB.
POOR: North America only, or nothing, even on FT8.

The ratings come from PropMon on the home server, worked out from solar flux, the K index and the time of day at the grid square named under the bands. They describe how open each band is in general, not a path to one place.

The heading shows the time of day the ratings use: DAWN, DAYTIME, DUSK or NIGHT.

Compare against on-air results: a few minutes on FT8 or a band scan tells you more than any rating.

SOLAR AND GEOMAGNETIC

SFI (solar flux): higher helps the upper bands.

K index: geomagnetic unrest, with NOAA's storm scale: 0–3 quiet, 4 unsettled, 5 storm G1, up to 9 storm G5. Storms close the higher bands first.

X-ray: solar flares. M and X flares can black out HF on the daylight side for minutes to hours (R1–R4).

Figures in amber are high enough to hurt conditions.

Storm recovery (amber line): after a storm PropMon holds the ratings down for a while, even once the K index has dropped.

WHEN DATA IS MISSING OR OLD

STALE · N MIN: no fresh ratings for 15 minutes or more. The page greys out; treat the ratings as old.

Band ratings unavailable: PropMon can't reach its rating source just now, so the bands show "--". The solar figures may still be current.

No data yet: PropMon has just restarted. Ratings return within a few minutes.

AWAY FROM HOME

On home sources the ratings describe the home grid square, wherever the console is. Field Mode will rate the bands for the operating site.

Console service not answering. Data on screen may be old. NWS alerts: check NOAA Weather Radio.
