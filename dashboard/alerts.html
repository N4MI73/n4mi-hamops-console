Help · AlertsWhat the Priority Card and this page show
THE PRIORITY CARD

Order, worst first: NWS warnings and lightning within 5 mi (red, flashing) → NWS alerts unavailable while storms are possible → watches → lightning forecast → a data source down → notable propagation → All quiet.

Flashing red border: a critical item. Acknowledge stops the flashing (it stops by itself after 2 minutes). The item stays on the card until it ends.

Several items: the card moves on every minute; ‹ › to look through them. A critical item holds the card.

THIS PAGE

On the Priority Card: everything the card can show, worst first. Details › gives the full NWS text and its instructions.

Notifications: less urgent items: heat, cold, other warnings, notices. "New" until you've seen them. They also light the badge on the Alerts button.

Ended · last 24 hours: NWS alerts that have ended, with the time.

Clear hold (lightning items, two taps): ends a lightning hold early when you know the storm has passed. The next strike starts it again.

WHEN DATA IS MISSING OR OLD

NWS alerts out of date / unavailable: the alert list can't be checked. Not the same as no alerts: use NOAA Weather Radio, named on the card.

"Can't tell while NWS alerts are out of date": the Ended list only counts an alert as ended while the NWS feed is current, so an outage can't make a live warning look finished.

SOUND

The console is visual only. NOAA Weather Radio is your audible alarm.

Console service not answering. Data on screen may be old. NWS alerts: check NOAA Weather Radio.
